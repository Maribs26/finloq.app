Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 5, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Finloq collects the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and account credentials
  • Financial Data: Income, expenses, transactions, and business records you input
  • Device Information: Device type, OS, and app version
  • Usage Analytics: How you use the app (anonymized and non-identifying)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data to:

  • Provide and maintain the Finloq app
  • Process your financial records and generate reports
  • Improve app performance and user experience
  • Send important security and account updates
  • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements

3. Data Security

Your financial data is protected through:

  • End-to-end encryption for data transmission
  • AES-256 encryption for data at rest
  • Regular security audits and penetration testing
  • Secure authentication and session management
  • Compliance with industry security standards

4. Data Sharing

We never share your financial data with third parties without your explicit consent, except:

  • When required by law or legal process
  • To service providers who help us operate the app (under strict confidentiality agreements)
  • With your explicit permission for export or integration

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data anytime
  • Request corrections to inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Export your financial records
  • Opt-out of non-essential communications

6. Data Retention

We retain your financial data for as long as your account is active. Upon account deletion, your data will be securely erased within 30 days, except where required by law.

7. GDPR & California Privacy Compliance

If you're in the EU or California, you have additional rights under GDPR and CCPA. Contact us to exercise these rights.

8. Cookies & Tracking

Finloq uses minimal cookies for authentication and security. We do not use tracking cookies or sell your data to advertisers.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy periodically. Significant changes will be notified via email or in-app notification.

10. Contact Us

For privacy concerns or questions, contact us at:

  • Email: privacy@finloq.app
  • Website: https://finloq.app
